erlic AI Research

Advancing Turkish-language artificial intelligence through efficient model architectures and open research.

Erlic AI is an independent AI research initiative focused on developing language models with a strong emphasis on Turkish language understanding, generation, and reasoning.

Research Areas

* Turkish Language Modeling: Developing language models optimized for Turkish linguistic structures and usage.
* Mixture-of-Experts (MoE): Exploring efficient sparse model architectures.
* Model Efficiency: Improving training and inference efficiency.
* Data Engineering: Building data preparation, filtering, and quality-control pipelines for Turkish text.
* Evaluation: Studying language understanding, generation, and reasoning capabilities.

Research Goals

Our goal is to contribute to Turkish-language AI research by exploring efficient model architectures, high-quality data pipelines, and reproducible experimentation.

We aim to make our research and selected project resources accessible to the broader AI research community.

Current Project Status

Erlic AI is currently in the engineering validation and compute-resource preparation phase.

The model architecture and training pipeline are under development and validation. Large-scale training has not yet been completed.

No trained model weights or benchmark results are being claimed at this stage.

Compute and Research Collaboration

We are preparing for large-scale model training and are interested in:

* GPU compute grants and research credits
* Access to high-performance GPU infrastructure
* Research partnerships
* Turkish language AI collaboration
